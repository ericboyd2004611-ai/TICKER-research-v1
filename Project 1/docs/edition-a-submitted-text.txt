Initial thesis
Watch / defer; do not initiate a base-case position yet. NuScale has an NRC-approved 77 MWe reactor-module design and a commercialization partner, but remains at an early commercial stage. Its revenues are small relative to cash use, and the valuation depends on binding customer commitments, deployable project economics, and avoidance of further material dilution. This is a starting hypothesis, not a price target or investment recommendation.

Consequential assumptions to test
1.   The 77 MWe design approval can translate into bankable project development and binding module sales rather than only preliminary commercial interest.
2.   NuScale can convert its ENTRA1 relationship into contracts with economics that produce positive long-run FCFF.
3.   The company can fund the path to commercial scale without share issuance that overwhelms per-share value creation.
4.   Cash and investments are available to shareholders after operating burn, restricted cash, contractual obligations, and required working capital are considered.
