# Peer policy

Recorded 2026-10-20T01:18:03.711779+00:00 before extracting peer valuation observations. This is a new policy, not a retroactively dated lab artifact.

Universe: US-listed nuclear reactor developers or nuclear equipment suppliers with public financial statements and a quoted September 1, 2026 close. Select by business exposure and stage, never by a desired multiple. Include OKLO as an early-stage advanced-reactor developer and BWXT as an established nuclear equipment supplier. Do not pool their multiples: the operating models, profitability, and stage differ. Exclude utilities (regulated owner/operator economics), uranium miners (commodity exposure), and diversified conglomerates (insufficient nuclear segment equity valuation).

Use dated shares outstanding rather than weighted-average EPS shares; June 30 reported statements available by September 1. EV = price times economic common shares + financial debt - cash and marketable investments; show lease/NCI and date limitations. EV/Sales uses trailing twelve months revenue, not annualized quarterly revenue. P/Book uses common stockholders equity. Mark nonpositive denominator multiples NM. Never hide an extreme but positive-denominator multiple. Show each peer separately and translate each multiple to SMR with the same bridge. No automatic peer median or fair-value target from this small heterogeneous set.

Explain any disagreement with the scenario DCF through current sales, dilution, excess liquidity, development options, profitability, and milestone timing.
