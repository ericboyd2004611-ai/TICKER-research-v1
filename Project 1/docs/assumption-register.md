# Assumption and control register

Financial judgments belong to the analyst who accepts or changes them. This register separates reported facts, derived observations, proposed choices and remaining personal decisions. It does not record approval that has not occurred. AI assistance is disclosed in validation-and-ai-use.md.

## Module price

Editable supplier revenue/module: $100–400m; central $200m, delayed $150m, stronger $250m. These are proposed stress choices. No public customer quote supports these endpoints. The upper stress covers the prior central reverse hurdle near $387m/module; the lower stress tests materially lower supplier revenue. The SEC PMA Section 1(g) redacts the negotiated price cap, so the application cannot verify that any selected price is legally/economically attainable. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1822966/000182296625000144/a101-partnershipmileston.htm. Proposed range and case values require personal acceptance, modification or rejection.

## Module margin

Editable margin before milestone fees: 0–60%; proposed cases 30%, 35%, 40%. Bounds preserve nonnegative pre-fee gross profit and allow an aggressive upside stress; they do not establish supplier economics. Historical SMR services margins are not evidence for mature module margins. Source context: June 2026 statements of operations and revenue discussion, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1822966/000182296626000085/smr-20260630.htm. Margin is measured before PMA fees, which are charged separately. These endpoints are analyst choices, not sourced confidence bounds.

## PMA escalation

Control 5–10%, lower bound anchored to Section 1(c)'s greater-of-5%-or-CPI rule. The 10% upper bound is a proposed inflation stress, not a contractual maximum. Inputs below 5% fail the source-based minimum check. Exact payment slots, invoice conditions and redacted cap remain unresolved. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1822966/000182296625000144/a101-partnershipmileston.htm

## Operating exit

Control 0–10× normalized next-year EBITDA, central 6×. Zero tests no operating sale; 10× is a judgmental upside endpoint. Public peer EV/sales and P/book provide context but do not validate an exit EBITDA multiple. The comparison is in peer-analysis.md. This is a proposed exit assumption and must not be described as an observed peer median.
